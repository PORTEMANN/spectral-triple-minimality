# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected behaviour:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the scientific community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behaviour:**
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Asserting metaphysical or esoteric claims as if they were mathematical proofs
- Dismissing rigorous criticism without engaging with its content

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at [contact@portemann.eu](mailto:contact@portemann.eu). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
